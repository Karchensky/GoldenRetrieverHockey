# Golden Retrievers — Precision Concepts 21–35

This batch uses only `GOLDEN RETRIEVERS`, `GR`, or no lettering. No new concept uses `GRH`.

All concepts use a black, white, and gold palette and were composed for team merchandise such as hoodies, shirts, hats, mugs, stickers, and patches.

| No. | Concept | Design focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 21 | Dense Heritage Seal | Full circular club seal with reduced empty space, a detailed retriever, and crossed taped hockey sticks |
| 22 | Majestic Border Sticks | Majestic retriever shield with two complete sticks integrated as structural side borders |
| 23 | Shield Blade Base | Retriever crest whose lower shield edge resolves into a clearly defined hockey-stick blade |
| 24 | Precise Store Lockup | Wide merchandise lockup with exact team name, dog medallion, and a separate regulation-shaped stick |
| 25 | Diagonal Carry Stick | Retriever carrying a complete diagonal stick with a distinct shaft, heel, taped blade, and puck |
| 26 | Art Deco GR | Corrected Art Deco crest using only a clear `GR`, a detailed retriever, and mirrored hockey sticks |
| 27 | Precision Speed Retriever | Simple running-retriever silhouette with controlled anatomy, speed bands, and puck |
| 28 | Dual Retriever Faceoff | Gold and white retrievers arranged around faceoff markings and paired pucks |
| 29 | Puck Medallion | Retriever portrait on the top face of a dimensional puck with a separate stick on the sidewall |
| 30 | Goal Frame Retriever | Retriever contained by a simplified hockey-goal frame with puck at the post |
| 31 | Precision GR Seal | Large, unmistakable `G` and `R`; retriever in the `R` counter; lower leg becomes a taped stick blade |
| 32 | Golden Retrievers Pennant | Vintage pennant with exact team name, dog medallion, lower-edge stick, and puck |

These are high-resolution raster concept images. The selected direction should be redrawn as vector artwork before final embroidery, screen printing, or large-format production.
